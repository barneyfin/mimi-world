MeeMee Rescue voice recording

Record each line once, in a quiet room, at the same distance from your headset microphone. The page trims the silence, brings every clip to the same loudness and saves it straight into the voice folder on your PC. Redo any line as often as you like.

1. Choose the voice folder

Pick Documents › Calude Code › Mimi World › voice. Chrome will ask to let this page save files there: choose Allow / Edit files. Recordings stay on your PC; nothing is uploaded.

2. Check the microphone

Say "MeeMee World" at your normal speaking voice. The bar should reach the middle, and never turn peach.

3. Record each line

Press Record, wait half a second, say the words, then press Stop. Keyboard: Space record or stop the highlighted line, P play it, ↑ ↓ move between lines. After each save the next line is highlighted.
